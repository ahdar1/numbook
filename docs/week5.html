1.3 Order of Operations: BODMAS rule

“Operations” refer to mathematical operations such as addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, taking power (\(a^n\)) or roots (\(\sqrt[n]{a}\)) (note: \(\sqrt[n]{a}=a^{1/n}\)).

1.3.1 Problem

How to work this out: 10:2(2+1) = ?

What to do first? The answer is BODMAS.

BODMAS = Bracket, Order, Division, Multiplication, Addition, and Subtraction.

Order of Operations and Priority
Symbol Meaning Operation Priority
B Bracket \(()\) 1
O Order \(a^n\) 1
D Division \(\div\) 2
M Multiplication \(\times\) 2
A Addition \(+\) 3
S Subtraction \(-\) 3
Important

You work out an expression from left to right in the order of BODMAS. Division and Multiplication are interchangeable (i.e., they have the same priority) and depend on which comes first in the expression. Similar rule applies for addition and subtraction as they have the same priority.

1.6 Greatest common factors (gcd)

The greatest common divisor (gcd) is also called the highest common factor (hcf). GCD of two numbers is the number that divides them both. For instance GCD of 3 and 6 is 3.

1.7 Lowest common factors (lcm)

The lowest common factors is also called the the lowest common multiple. LCM of number A (say 2) and B (say 5) is the lowest number that can be divided by A and B without a remainder. For example, the lcm for 2 and 5 is 10 because 10 can be divided by 2 and 5 without a remainder.
