Outline

Author

My name is Ahmad Daryanto (Anto). I am the module leader and developed this website. This is my personal website https://sites.google.com/view/ahmaddaryanto/

Aim

At the end of the course, I want you to say this: I love marketing and don’t avoid numbers.

Learning Objectives

  1. To improve basic numeracy skills and understand basic statistical concepts (statistics in MKTG200)

  2. To increase students’ appreciation of the importance of gaining those skills in the marketing environment

  3. As a preparation for advanced modules in Part 2

Textbook

No textbook is needed. But if you want to buy a book, I recommend Daryanto (2020). This is a compilation book (I don’t create it!) in which the chapters are taken from Croft, Croft, and Davison (2006) and Berenson et al. (2012) (Honestly, this is my quickest route of having a book with my name on it). I used this book in the past but realized that students were complaining the book was too thin to put under their pillows.

Excel

I assume you know how to use Excel to calculate a basic mathematical operation e.g., 1+1. To answer certain problems in this manual, I provide Excel commands. This is a nice tool to use but also dangerous. Without an understanding, the use of Excel can put you in the dark forever. Make sure you understand how to solve the problems manually before using Excel.
