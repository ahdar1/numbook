1.1 Fraction and Decimal Number

Express 0.142 as a proper fraction in its simplest form. \(1\frac{3}{200}\)

  1. Express 0.015 as a proper fraction in its simplest form. \(\frac{83}{250}\)

  2. Express 4.6 as a mixed fraction in its simplest form. \(4\frac{3}{5}\)

  3. Write \(\frac{1}{100}+\frac{5}{1000}\) as a decimal number. 0.015

  4. Round 24.362 to 3 decimal places (3 dp). 24.4

  5. Round 0.0452 to 2 dp. 0.05

  6. In this fraction \(\frac{1}{6}\), the numerator is 1 and the denominator is 6.

  3. Write 0.0473 to 1 sf. 0.05
