1.2 Order or Operations: BODMAS rule

1.2.1 Problem

How to work this out: 5+2(2+4) = ?

1.2.2 Concept

BODMAS = Bracket over Division, Multiplication, Addition, and Subtraction.

Order of Operations and Priority
Symbol Operation Priority
B \(()\) 1
D \(\div\) 2
A \(+\) 3
M \(\times\) 3
M \(-\) 3
