Week 7a

Learning goals

  • Understand the meaning of percentages and ratio.
  • Know how to perform calculation with percentages and ratio.
  • Know how to calculate percentage changes.

Percentages and Ratio

Problem

Transform 3.6 into a percentage.

Watch

Exercises

  1. Transform 2.5 into a percentage.

  2. Calculate 12% of 60.

  3. Calculate 11.4% of 100.

Calculating Percentages Changes

Problem

Last month, 250 consumers bought a new smartphone. This month, the number is 420. What is the percentage change in sale?

Watch

Exercises

  1. Last month, 250 consumers bought a new smartphone. This month, the number is down to 100. What is the percentage change in sale?

  2. Yesterday there were 250 unique visitors clicking a website. Today, you found that there is a 5% increase in visit. How many clicks are there today?
