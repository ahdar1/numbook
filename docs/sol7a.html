1.3 Calculating Percentages Changes

Problems

  • Last month, 250 consumers bought a new smartphone. This month, the number is 420. What is the percentage increase in sale?

\(\frac{420-250}{250} \times 100 \% =\frac{170}{250} \times 100 \% =68 \%\)

  • This year, your monthly rent is £750. Next year,the monthly rent is reduced by 5%. Calculate the new rent.

The new rent is \((100 \% - 5 \%) \times £ 750 = 95 \% \times £750 = £712.5\)

Exercises

  1. Last month, 250 consumers bought a new smartphone. This month, the number is down to 100. What is the percentage decrease in sale?

\(\frac{100-250}{250} \times 100 \% =\frac{150}{250} \times 100 \% = -0.6 \ 100% = -60 \%\). In an absolute term, the percentage reduction is 60%.

  1. Yesterday there were 250 unique visitors clicking a website. Today, you found that there is a 10% increase in visit. How many clicks are there today?

\(110 \% \times 250 = 275\)
