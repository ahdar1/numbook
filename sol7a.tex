\hypertarget{calculating-percentages-changes}{%
\subsection*{1.3 Calculating Percentages
Changes}\label{calculating-percentages-changes}}
\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{1.3 Calculating Percentages Changes}

\textbf{Problems}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  Last month, 250 consumers bought a new smartphone. This month, the
  number is 420. What is the percentage increase in sale?
\end{itemize}

\(\frac{420-250}{250} \times 100 \% =\frac{170}{250} \times 100 \% =68 \%\)

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  This year, your monthly rent is £750. Next year,the monthly rent is
  reduced by 5\%. Calculate the new rent.
\end{itemize}

The new rent is
\((100 \% - 5 \%) \times £ 750 = 95 \% \times £750 = £712.5\)

\textbf{Exercises}

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  Last month, 250 consumers bought a new smartphone. This month, the
  number is down to 100. What is the percentage decrease in sale?
\end{enumerate}

\(\frac{100-250}{250} \times 100 \% =\frac{-150}{250} \times 100 \% = -0.6 \times 100 \% = -60 \%\).
In an absolute term, the percentage reduction is 60\%.

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\setcounter{enumi}{1}
\tightlist
\item
  Yesterday there were 250 unique visitors clicking a website. Today,
  you found that there is a 10\% increase in visit. How many clicks are
  there today?
\end{enumerate}

\(110 \% \times 250 = 275\)
